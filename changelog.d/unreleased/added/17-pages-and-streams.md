- **Glue for pages that load in place, live streams, and escaping tests.** `address`, `history` and `go` keep a page loaded in place on its address; `View` reads an address's query leniently; `clear` empties a region; `paced` spaces a live stream's changes and ends it after a lifetime, and `Last` sends a region only when it differs; `escaping::Guard` checks answers escape text exactly once. `reopen` now escapes its URL with numeric references, as askama does, and refuses a URL holding a control or space character, which a browser strips into another host.
