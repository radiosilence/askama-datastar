- **`reopen(url)`: a live stream that reopens itself.** Datastar does not reopen a stream that ended, and cannot see one whose socket went silent, so pages wrote reopen attributes by hand. `reopen` writes them for an element that stays on the page: the stream is opened again once it ends or has said nothing for 40 seconds, not after a 4xx until the page is shown again, and the URL is read from `data-url` rather than written into an expression.
